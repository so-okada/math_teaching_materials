Exterior coating volume
Volume still missing from the infinite horn\(\pi/L\to 0\)

\[V_{\mathrm{inside}}(L)=\pi\int_1^L\frac{dx}{x^2}=\pi\left(1-\frac1L\right).\]

\[V_{\mathrm{coat}}(L,t)=\pi\int_1^L\left[\left(\frac1x+t\right)^2-\frac1{x^2}\right]dx=2\pi t\ln L+\pi t^2(L-1).\]

For every fixed \(t>0\), the coating volume grows without bound as \(L\to\infty\), while the inside volume tends to \(\pi\).
