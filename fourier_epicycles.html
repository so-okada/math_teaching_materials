Drawing a Closed Curve with Fourier Epicycles

A closed plane curve can be parametrized by a continuous, 1-periodic complex-valued function z(t) = x(t) + i y(t), with z(t + 1) = z(t). For the piecewise-smooth parametrizations used here, z(t) equals its Fourier series:

z(t)  =  ∞Σk = −∞ ak e2πikt,     ak  =  ∫01 z(t) e−2πikt dt.

In this demo, the curve is sampled at N = 511 points zn = z(n/N), equally spaced along its length. The discrete coefficients are ck = (1/N) N−1Σn = 0 zn e−2πikn/N for integers k with −255 ≤ k ≤ 255. They approximate the continuous coefficients ak above.
