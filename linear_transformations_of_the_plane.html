Linear Transformations of the Plane

How a \(2\times 2\) matrix moves every point of \(\mathbb{R}^2\)

$$ T(\vec v) \;=\; A\vec v, \qquad A\begin{pmatrix}x\\[1pt] y\end{pmatrix} = x\,(A\vec e_1) + y\,(A\vec e_2) $$

The columns of \(A\) are the images \(A\vec e_1,\;A\vec e_2\) of the standard basis vectors \(\vec{e}_{1}\) and \(\vec{e}_{2}\).
