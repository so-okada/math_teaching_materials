The Sampling Theorem: Hearing Aliasing

In this idealized model, a digital recorder stores uniformly spaced samples of a sound wave, taken fs times per second. The Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem says that the signal can be reconstructed exactly when all its frequencies are below fs/2.
